## Delivered interface

- Local **I dag / Historikk / Oppsett** (**Today / History / Settings**) tabs. Today is the default. Arrow keys, Home and End move and select tabs; the selected tab and its panel are semantically associated. These use local state and add no browser-history entries or database writes.
- All three panels remain mounted while hidden, retaining entry, goal and custom-activity drafts and history pagination. Manage goals activates Settings and focuses the goal heading. Historical editing activates Today, populates the existing entry and focuses its amount. An ongoing edit has an explicit Continue editing action in History; goal quick-log and other entry-edit actions are disabled until it is saved or cancelled.
- Goal quick-log selects the exact activity/unit, keeps a deliberately selected date, focuses the amount and scrolls it above fixed mobile navigation. The automatic date still follows the current day. Saving remains manual and retains the duplicate-submit and authoritative-write safeguards.
- Removed introductory filler and repeated goal-status helpers. History has compact participation/current-streak/longest-streak metrics, totals, entries and the seven-day overview. Dates use the programme locale; singular/plural wording is corrected. Unknown historical goal status is omitted from rows, while ACT-01's underlying distinction remains unchanged.
- Native disclosures provide Activity Level, historical goals, custom-activity and reminder information. Goal targets use measurement-specific labels. Reminders retain the existing timing and persistence; their wording explains the tab-only limit without implying monitoring of other applications or physical movement.
- One local status region reports saving and authoritative success. Success expires after 3.5 seconds, is replaced/restarted by another action, and clears when changing sections. Its timer cleans up on unmount. Errors persist, retain input and are announced as alerts. Feedback is no longer sticky.
- Scoped CSS preserves the RPG theme and desktop two-column Today layout, gives progress percentages a nonwrapping column, and adds focus/scroll spacing. No animation, framework or dependency was added.

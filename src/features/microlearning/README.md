# Microlearning (bounded Lab)

An additive, immersive practice surface at `/rpg/micro` (no scrolling at normal text size) for spare minutes. It does not replace or change the Campaign: lessons, authentic exercises, coding, checkpoints and mastery all stay where they are. It renders whatever curated sessions the active programme ships (`ProgrammeContent.microlearning`); a programme with none gets a short notice and a way back.

## What it is

- **Six card families**, each a renderer under `components/`: Learn, Select, Order (tap to place), Match (tap left, then right), Tap the problem, Assemble (tap tokens). They are taken from the programme's own card data (`MicroCard` in `@/lib/domain`), never from fixtures here.
- **Two intents.** «Forbered deg» is one short primer for what comes next in the Campaign (an advance organizer: one idea, one example, one scaffolded check, two minutes; the Campaign mission is still where the material is learned). «Repeter» is retrieval of what the learner has already worked on. Both are the same session model: `SessionPicker` lists the open ones in two groups; `SessionRunner` plays one in order. Nothing is random, adaptive or scheduled.
- **Availability is the Campaign's own position, read-only.** What opens is derived from the same read model that drives Home and the Map (`loadRpgCampaignPosition` in `@/features/rpg`: required quests in authored order, which are complete or reached, the current quest) and the pure functions in `@/lib/domain/microlearning.ts`. A «Repeter» session opens when every Campaign item its own cards cite has been started or completed. A primer is in range when its missions are not all complete, it starts no later than the required quest after the current one, and it does not end before the current one; at most one is offered, and the one covering the current quest wins. Nothing is hard-coded to module numbers, `/rpg/micro` applies the same rules to a direct visit, and an unreadable Campaign opens nothing. The whole answer comes from one function, `loadRpgMicroAvailability` (`@/features/rpg`), which both the route and Home call. Some Campaign phases (the Lab, the Oppdrag) honestly have neither a primer nor a «Repeter» session; that sparse availability is intended, and no content exists merely to fill it.
- **One retry loop for every assessed card** (`model.ts`, `PracticeFrame`): a first miss shows a hint and offers `Prøv igjen`; a second miss shows the answer and allows Next. No hearts, penalties, XP or forced repetition. Learn cards are not assessed: no answer, no check, no retry. Each ends in a bridge that names the practice that follows.
- **An immersive shell.** The route is a sibling of `RpgShell` under `RpgRouteShell`: theme, startup and the onboarding redirect still apply, but the six-item navigation and the hero HUD never render.
- **The hard viewport contract.** One card is one viewport: 360 × 560 CSS px, no page scroll, no horizontal scroll, no nested scroll, no clipped content, every control in view and at least 44 × 44 px. The interaction is anchored next to the footer, and its targets grow on a tall phone, so a tall screen does not leave a gap above the footer. `e2e/microlearning.spec.ts` drives every card of the real bank through every state at 360 × 560 and at 384 × 800 and fails if one ever scrolls or clips.
- **Larger text scrolls the route, never clips.** The contract above is for normal text size. When the learner's text is larger, accessibility wins over zero scroll: nothing below the route clips, scrolls, pins or has a fixed height, so the route (`.micro`, the one scroll context) simply grows past the viewport and scrolls, with the footer after the content in reading order. The Match grid drops to one column when two would be too narrow, and a new card starts scrolled to its top. The same walk runs with text scaled by automated stress proxies (font-size tokens at 125 % and 150 %, root font size at 200 %); they are stress tests, not a standards result or a substitute for a device font scale.

## What it must not do

It holds one piece of state per card (where it is in the retry loop and what is placed or chosen) and one per session (the card index), in memory. It writes no evidence, mastery, XP, achievement, Campaign completion, streak or scheduling data, and uses no IndexedDB, `localStorage` or `sessionStorage`. It plays no audio and uses no speech API. It never reads app state: the app layer (`src/app/routes/MicrolearningRoute.tsx`) reads the active programme's scope and the Campaign position (a read) and hands the answer in. The only other way in is a secondary link on Home (`RpgHomePage`). Home asks `loadRpgMicroAvailability` (the very function behind the picker; a read, nothing written, after the quest has rendered), and only a programme that ships a bank is asked. It shows the link only while a primer or a «Repeter» session is open; when none is, it shows a plain line ("Ingen mikroøkt akkurat nå…") and no link, and while loading or after a failed read it shows nothing, so it never promises a session that is not there. There is no navigation item.

`tests/architecture/microlearningBoundary.test.ts` pins this mechanically (the import allowlist, type-only domain imports, and the forbidden storage, network, app-state, audio and speech names). `tests/app/microlearningRoute.test.tsx` snapshots every learner store before and after whole sessions, right and wrong, and a negative control proves the snapshot notices a write.

## Adding content

A programme owns its bank (for Programvareutvikling: `src/programmes/programvareutvikling/microlearning/`): `sessions` for «Repeter» and optional `primers` for «Forbered deg», each naming the Campaign items (`prepares`) it is for. A primer reuses the very card objects of a session where it can (a Learn card and the scaffolded check its bridge names) and adds cards of its own only where nothing existed. The validator in `@/lib/domain` fails the programme definition closed when a card cites a module the programme lacks, a Learn card has no bridge or no practice after it, an Order or Match is shown already solved, and so on. Every interactive title must say what to do ("Hva skrives ut?", "Sett trinnene i riktig rekkefølge"), not name the topic. Keep a hint short and not the answer, and a card short enough for the viewport.

## Not in scope here

Persistence, a scheduler, spaced repetition, adaptive selection, a larger item bank, audio, speech, mistake history, a global navigation change, a walking or context mode, and any Epic F or G work.
